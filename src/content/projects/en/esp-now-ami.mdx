## Context and problem

Demonstrate, with real hardware, a line code studied in the course: pseudo-ternary AMI, in which information is carried by pulses of alternating polarity, and not by two fixed levels.

## Architecture and technical decisions

```ascii title="Topology"
 [Slave 1] --ESP-NOW--+
                      +--> [Master] --USB Serial--> PC: website/ (:3000)
 [Slave 2] --ESP-NOW--+

 PC slave --USB Serial--> [Slave n]    website-slave/ (:3001, :3002)
```

- **Encoding:** bit `0` alternates between $+V$ and $-V$; bit `1` stays at $0$ V. Alternating the polarity of the zeros keeps the average signal level at zero.
- **Symmetric XOR cipher** applied to the message before encoding, to show the encrypted and decrypted message in the interface.
- **One firmware for both slaves**, distinguished by `SLAVE_ID` as a build flag in `platformio.ini`. The master's MAC also comes in as a build flag.

The master is an ESP32-S3 SuperMini that only routes between ESP-NOW and Serial. On the PC, each slave's interface shows a waveform preview while the message is typed; the master's shows, per slave, the AMI waveform, the decoded bits, the bytes in hexadecimal, and the encrypted and decrypted message.
