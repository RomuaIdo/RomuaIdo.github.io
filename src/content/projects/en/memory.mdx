## Context and problem

Design a complete digital system in hardware, not just isolated blocks: the game lights a pattern on the board's 10 LEDs and the player must reproduce it on the switches before time runs out.

## Architecture and technical decisions

```ascii title="Top-level blocks (Memory.vhd)"
 switches, buttons -> +-----------------+ ---> LEDs (pattern)
 Leds (PRNG) -------> | Game_Controller |
 Timer -------------> |      (FSM)      |
                      +--------+--------+
                               |
                               v
              Bin_to_dec / Bin_to_dec_2dig
                               |
                               v
             7-segment displays
             (points, lives, speed)
```

- **Randomness from reaction time:** a high-frequency counter runs free, and its value is captured at the instant of the player's interaction. It generates unpredictable 10-bit patterns without an LFSR.
- **Parametric timer:** a frequency divider controls how long the pattern stays visible; the speed increases after every three consecutive correct answers.
- **Score proportional to speed** and three lives, shown on 7-segment displays (active low).

## Implementation

The game logic is a state machine with `START`, `GEN_PATTERN`, `SHOW_LEDS`, `WAIT_INPUT`, `CHECK_RESULT`, and `GAME_OVER`.
