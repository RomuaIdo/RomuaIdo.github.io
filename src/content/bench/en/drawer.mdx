## Problem

With dozens of component drawers, finding where a 10 kΩ 0805 resistor is, or how many regulators are left, became a manual search.

## Solution

Desktop app that represents the cabinet as blocks of drawers (4×4, 3×3 or 1×1), each drawer with a size and items.

- Items with category, value and package; the same item can be in several drawers and the quantity is summed.
- Minimum stock per item: below the limit, the drawers get a visual warning right on the cabinet drawing.
- Pick and restock lists applied to the stock in one go.
- Draggable blocks with grid snapping and overlap detection; multiple selection.
- Export of the mapping to CSV and built-in electronics and mechanics tools.

## Implementation

C++17 with Qt 6 (Widgets, Sql and Test) and CMake. The data lives in a SQLite database in the app's data directory. The domain logic is a separate library (`drawer_core`), tested with Qt Test via `ctest`.
